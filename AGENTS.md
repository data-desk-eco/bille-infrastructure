# Bille gas seepage: research log

Notebook: `docs/index.html` (published at research.datadesk.eco/bille-infrastructure, access list in `access.conf`).
Question: where is the gas that has bubbled up through creeks, boreholes and compounds in Bille Town (Degema LGA, Rivers State, 4.5763 N 6.8888 E) since late October 2025 coming from, and what changed in the area at that time?

## What the sources say

- Onset: fishermen saw bubbling with a sulphurous smell at Opu Osia, on the creek south of the town, on or about 25 October 2025; within a week it appeared at sites inside the town (Amnesty UA 20/26; SOMO brief). Over 74 seep points by June 2026 (Premium Times). Gas exits through abandoned mono pumps, shallow wells and boreholes; borehole water turns black on standing (crude or condensate in the aquifer).
- Measurements: NOSDRA, December 2025: methane 10,000 times background at one site; methane, VOCs and H2S in air; methane above 36% LEL (Social Action). NUPRC, July 2026: methane along a 1.5 km stretch; the first aquifer is contaminated; "no relationship between any pipeline or facility and the character of the gas seepage"; "a subsurface occurrence rather than a failure of surface infrastructure"; gas "migrating from deep underground reservoirs" through groundwater pathways and boreholes. An international firm was engaged for fieldwork from late July 2026; no result is public as of 3 September 2026.
- Operators in the joint investigation: NNPC Eighteen Operating Ltd (OML 18, replaced Eroton in 2023; partners NNPC 55%, Eroton, OML 18 Energy Resource / Sahara, Bilton), Newcross E&P (OML 24: Awoba, Awoba NW, Ekulama; the Awoba unit straddles OMLs 18 and 24) and Renaissance Africa Energy (ex-SPDC: EGGS-1 gas line, Soku).

## What the data shows

1. Wells. Sixteen Krakama field wells sit 2.3 to 6.1 km south and south-east of the town, completed 1958 to 1992 by Shell; Krakama-1 (1958) and Bille-1 (1971, 3.1 km north-east) are plugged and abandoned; Minama-1 (1967) is 5.9 km north. MapStand still marks the other Krakama wells as producing, but the Krakama flow station flare (VIIRS site 2405, 2.8 km) has been dark since June 2024 and NUPRC lists no Krakama production. Troops arrested people tampering with Krakama wellhead 12 in April 2025, and crude tapping on Akaso well 14/17 flowlines in the "Tuma and Krakrama" area in January 2025.
2. Pipelines. The Nembe Creek Trunk Line (oil, reportedly flushed and disused), Krakama spur 1 (oil) and EGGS-1 (Renaissance gas line to Bonny NLNG) pass 0.8 to 1.0 km from the town centre. Processed gas in EGGS-1 or GTS-4 would not carry the H2S, VOCs and crude that residents observe, so the pipeline hypothesis is weak on composition alone; NUPRC says the same.
3. Timing. Three things changed in the year before the seeps: the Awoba unit restarted on 13 April 2024 and VIIRS flaring at the Awoba flow station (9 km west) resumed the same month; Newcross prepared gas compressors on OML 24 in early 2025 and restored Ekulama-16L (shut in since 1993); OML 18 exports moved to the FSO Cawthorne, commissioned 6 October 2025, with a 50 kb/d exit target. NOSDRA logged an equipment failure with a crude release at Awoba well 6 (Eroton-era OML 18 well, 7.2 km west of the town) on 16 October 2025, nine days before the first seep report. Awoba flaring rose from October 2025.
4. Precedent in OML 18. Idle Shell-era wells in the same lease have failed repeatedly: Alakiri 9T (fire, February to December 2024), Akaso 4 at Krakrama (fire, August 2024 to January 2025) and Buguma well 8 (leak from 1 December 2024, blowout 21 December, fire 12 January to 12 February 2025). EMIT saw a 1,918 kg/h methane plume 350 m from Buguma well 8 on 18 December 2024, three days before the blowout, and 565 kg/h at Buguma Creek wells 3/10 on 23 October 2024. The archive holds both (IMEO rows) and the notebook overlays the 18 December enhancement.
5. Hyperspectral at Bille. EMIT crossed Bille once after the onset, on 21 February 2026 at 83% cloud. Enhancement reaches about 1,100 ppm·m over the western part of the town (4.5785 N 6.8869 E), where the seeps cluster, and 1,150 ppm·m 200 m from Krakama-14/16. The 99th percentile of the scene is 630 ppm·m, so these are two-sigma pixels, not detections. Carbon Mapper's Tanager has no scene over Bille. Ask Carbon Mapper (Source International asked for an introduction) and Planet for tasking.
6. Sentinel-1. `scripts/s1.py` caches 20 m VV/VH windows (lon 6.80 to 6.96, lat 4.50 to 4.64) for all 453 RTC scenes since January 2024; `scripts/composites.py` writes the per-site peak-backscatter series that the notebook plots. No Krakama well, Bille-1, Minama-1 or the Krakama flow station shows a persistent new bright target (a rig, barge or new structure holds +6 dB or more for weeks) between August 2025 and March 2026, so a drilling or workover operation next to the town in the weeks before the seeps is unlikely. Krakama-12 shows single-pass bright targets on four dates between 24 April and 29 July 2025, after the April tampering arrests. Awoba-5 (Newcross) holds +6 to +10 dB from 2 March 2026 onward, i.e. work at that well after the seeps began. Akaso-4 was bright in February to March 2024 and again from March 2026; Akaso-14 from June 2026. Krakama-1, -2 and -5 sit in water and their spikes are boats.
7. Sentinel-2. `scripts/s2.py` caches 20 m windows for the 276 tile-32NKL scenes since October 2022 with tile cloud below 85% (the AWS COGs already carry the BOA offset: reflectance is DN/10000). `scripts/composites.py` writes the dry-season NDVI change (November to April, 2025-26 against 2023-25), per-scene NDVI and water-reflectance series within 1.5 km of the seeps against a 4 to 6 km control ring, and vegetation-loss clusters. `scripts/rgb.py` writes true-colour crops of the town for the clearest scene of each dry season as map layers. Result: the mean NDVI of vegetation within 1.5 km of the seeps minus the control ring was -0.023 over 57 scenes from October 2022 to October 2024 and -0.022 over 24 scenes in the year before onset, and -0.059 over 16 scenes from November 2025 to August 2026 (single months reach -0.08 to -0.15 in January, February, June and August 2026). That is a relative greening deficit of about 0.035 NDVI near the seeps since onset, consistent with the mangrove dieback residents report, with the caveat that the near area includes the town and cloud masks vary. Water red reflectance shows no difference. The scene-centred change map has loss clusters of 0.5 to 1.9 ha on the western edge of the town and the creek bank to its north-west, where the seeps are, but the largest clusters (7 to 18 ha) lie 5 to 11 km away near Idama and west of Awoba, so dieback is not unique to Bille.

## Reading of the evidence

Ranked by fit with the composition (H2S, VOCs, crude in boreholes), the spatial pattern (many points along 1.5 km, exiting through boreholes) and the timing:

1. Gas charging the shallow aquifer from a well with lost integrity, most likely one of the 1958 to 1966 Krakama wells or Bille-1, driven by pressure changes as the Awoba/Krakama trend was brought back on stream and gas handling changed on OML 24 in 2025. This fits NUPRC's own wording ("deep reservoir", "groundwater pathways and boreholes") better than a natural seep, which would not start on a date.
2. Damage to a Krakama wellhead by crude thieves (documented at wellhead 12 in April 2025 and on the Akaso flowlines), letting gas into the annulus and shallow sands. Same mechanism as 1 with a different trigger.
3. A shallow gas pocket disturbed by borehole drilling in the town. Does not explain the sulphur, crude staining or the creek seeps 600 m outside the town.
4. A pipeline. Composition argues against it; distance (0.8 km) does not rule it out for the NCTL, which carried crude and is disused.

Tests that would decide it: δ13C and C2+ composition of seep gas against Krakama and Awoba produced gas (Source International can sample; NNPC holds the reference); sustained casing pressure and annulus surveys at Krakama-1 to -16 and Bille-1; a soil-gas grid between the town and the Krakama wells; NUPRC's well files for any 2025 intervention; Newcross and NEOL production and injection by well for August to November 2025.

## Data and pipeline

- `make etl`: MapStand pipelines and wells, NOSDRA spills, archive extracts (`scripts/fetch_archive.sql`), Sentinel-2 and Sentinel-1 caches, composites. The caches are gitignored (about 300 MB); the products in `data/s2`, `data/s1`, `data/*.parquet` and `docs/assets` are committed.
- `make data`: `scripts/load.sql` builds `data/data.duckdb` from committed inputs. Distances are from the town node.
- `data/events.csv` is the hand-compiled timeline with sources; extend it as the case develops.
- `data/emit.json` and `docs/assets/emit_*.png` are windowed EMIT L2B CH4ENH v002 granules (Earthdata login in `~/.netrc`).
- Planet tasking and the Data Desk Planet skill need a fresh `gcloud auth login`; not used here.
- Blocked sources: several Nigerian news sites return 403 to the fetch tool; `curl` with a browser user agent and `trafilatura` worked for Premium Times and Sahara Reporters, not for Vanguard, TheCable, Guardian NG or Channels.
